--- layout: default title: "bundle fund" description: "Lists information about gems seeking funding assistance" url: /bundler-reference/bundle-fund permalink: /bundler-reference/bundle-fund/ previous: /bundler-reference/bundle-exec next: /bundler-reference/bundle-gem --- Lists information about gems seeking funding assistance 
bundle fund [OPTIONS]

Description

bundle fund lists information about gems seeking funding assistance.

Options

--group=<list>, -g=<list>
Fetch funding information for a specific group.

Examples

# Lists funding information for all gems
bundle fund

# Lists funding information for a specific group
bundle fund --group=security
