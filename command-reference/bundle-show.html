This reference was automatically generated from Bundler version 4.0.18.

bundle show [GEM]
              [--paths]

Description

Without the [GEM] option, show will print a list of the names and versions of all gems that are required by your Gemfile(5), sorted by name.

Calling show with [GEM] will list the exact location of that gem on your machine.

Options

--paths
List the paths of all gems that are required by your Gemfile(5), sorted by gem name.
